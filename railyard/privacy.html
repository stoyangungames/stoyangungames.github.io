Privacy Policy — Railyard

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Railyard (the "game"), an Android game published by StoyanGun Games ("we", "us").

Contact: stoyangungames@gmail.com

Summary

  • The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not collect, receive or store any personal information about you.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to deliver and measure those ads, as described below.

Information we collect

None. The game does not send us any data, and we operate no servers for it.

Your game progress — levels cleared, coins, boosters, lives, daily streak and settings — is stored in a file on your device. It never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and funded by ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. To show and measure ads, the AdMob software inside the game may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate (not precise) location;
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language and screen size;
  • information about the ads shown and how you interact with them;
  • diagnostic information such as crash and performance data related to ads.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, and detect fraud. Depending on your choices and your region, Google may also use it to show ads personalised to your interests.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Your choices

  • Consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland). When you first open the game in these regions, you are asked whether you consent to the use of your data for personalised advertising. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Options → Ad privacy choices. If you do not consent, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are optional. Ads that give a reward (extra time, extra coins, a hint, an extra life, a streak save) are shown only when you choose to watch them.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help address it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as access, correction, deletion or objection to processing. Because we do not hold any personal data about you, requests about advertising data should be made to Google using the tools and links above. You can also contact us at the address above and we will help where we can.

Data security and retention

We hold no personal data, so we have nothing to retain. Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Google's retention of advertising data is described in Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date. If the game starts handling data in a new way, the policy will be updated before that change is released.
